FOOTBALL WARS — ROAD TO GLORY · CHANGELOG
Source: FootballWars_Arcade_Mayhem.html (Arcade Mayhem V2)  →  FootballWars_Full_Test.html (+ FootballWars_Full_Test_itch.zip)
The earlier builds FootballWars_Arcade.html and FootballWars_Arcade_Mayhem.html are left unchanged in the repository.

CORE / CAREER
- Create Your Club replaces the real-club picker: name (3–22), short name (2–5), 3 crest shapes, 8 original pixel crest
  symbols, 2 kit colours, starting formation. Editable later for free (Club → Edit). Stored as save.profile;
  the player's club uses the permanent id PLAYER_CLUB everywhere (divisions, fixtures, table, battle team).
- 199 rival source clubs + your club fill the 10 Divisions (20 each); the 200th source club is kept as the guest of the
  new Glory Cup (one extra card per Division on the Challenge Board).
- Three formations with real role counts: Balanced 16/14/12/34/8/15/1, All-Out Attack 22/20/14/25/7/11/1,
  Iron Fortress 12/10/10/44/10/13/1. Raw HP and damage-per-second budgets are equalised; a small measured
  effectiveness calibration (Attack ×0.96, Fortress ×0.98) keeps win rates level in same-seed probes. Each formation
  brings its own two V4 team signature moves.
- Five permanent Training stats (Lv 1–30, same cost curve) unchanged.
- ARC_CFG power-gap curve softened from 0.35/18 to 0.25/22 (Powers and AI stars now add strength on both sides).
  Snapshots store the values they were started with; pre-Glory snapshots keep 0.35/18.
- Rival clubs field Division-appropriate stars in league and AI-vs-AI matches (D10–8: 0, D7–5: 1, D4–2: 2, D1: 3).

AUTO POWER MAYHEM (engine stage 3: tools/patch_glory.py + tools/engine_glory_ext.js)
- Manual War Commands (Power Kick / Team Rush / Iron Defense buttons, energy bar, targeting) removed from the UI;
  Momentum Strike does not return. Player and AI teams run under identical automatic Power rules.
- 24 Powers with their own id, trigger condition, real effect, cooldown, event (EV.POWER 90), visual and sound:
  Attack: Rocket Boots, Pinball Strike, Wing Stampede, Golden Volley, Berserker Attack, Meteor Shot
  Defense: Fortress, Counter Shield, Interception Net, Iron Curtain, Miracle Save, Last Stand
  Chaos: Domino Impact, Wild Ball, Bowling Charge, Thunder Tackle, Turbo Madness, Ricochet Fever
  Support: Second Wind, Captain's Rally, Medical Rush, Adrenaline Wave, Tactical Genius, Star Spotlight
  Shot Powers use the real match ball (SHOOTING state, keeper saves, blocks, posts, wide, GOAL); Pinball / Ricochet
  bounce the real ball; Wild Ball changes real loose-ball physics; movement Powers use real runs and collisions.
- Loadout per match: 2 Favorites + 2 Wilds (rarity weights 60/28/10/2 on the unlocked collection, re-normalised,
  4 different Powers, max 1 Legendary), fixed in the battle snapshot. First chance ~6–9 s, then every ~8–12 s,
  Favorite/Wild 50/50 unless a real scoring chance is on, same Power ≥10 s apart, ≤2 active Power effects per team,
  a Power that cannot apply is not consumed. Miracle Save and Last Stand react to real moments.
- Power Mastery: rank 2 at 15 successful uses (+5 %), rank 3 at 40 (+10 %), scaling each Power's own parameters.
  Counted once per use, from official fixtures (final result only) and first Challenge wins; never from REMATCH
  first attempts, repeat Challenges or Promotion Challenge retries.
- Unlocks: 6 starters; Team Level 4/6/8/12/16/20/23; Divisions 9/8/6/4/2/1; 25 career ball wins; 10 career goals;
  D5 Signature Showdown first win; first D1 title; Team Level 35 + Division 5.
- Kept from Mayhem: goal reinforcements (+3 per goal, max +9, ≤100 alive, never stars), no zone, no storm,
  Final Frenzy at 40 s, chain collision, KO Rampage, counterattack alerts, time-up decided without K.O.s.
  The +6 % V4 goal buff stays off.

STARS / DRAFT / ACADEMY
- Stars are permanent (no contracts): Stars.canEquip/consume/cleanupExpired/battleList, Draft.open/pick and all
  contract UI were changed or removed. Max 5 active; role limits follow the chosen formation.
- Star Mastery: Rookie 0 / Rising 8 / Skilled 24 / Veteran 48 / Club Legend 80 XP → +0 / 0.75 / 1.5 / 2.5 / 3.5
  effective OVR and +0–8 % signature ability power (both really used by the battle unit). Star XP: official win +3,
  loss +1 (final result), Challenge first win +2.
- League Adaptation: an active star with 6 final official matches in a Division adapts to that Division's rarity floor −2
  (one time per Division, never weaker, ≤99).
- Draft: same packs, prices, odds and pity. Duplicates give Star XP (6/9/13/18) — or Pack Fragments (2/3/5/8) for a
  Club Legend — shown before signing.
- Contract Tickets became Academy Training Tickets (+5 / +10 Star XP).

PROGRESSION / REWARDS
- Mobile League Milestones after matches 4 / 9 / 14 (+2 F, +2 F, +2 F + Academy +5); D10–D2 once per Division,
  D1 every Endless season. Free Standard Pack after match 3 kept. Founder's Bonus +45 C once (first final result).
- Coach Analysis after losses (≤3 tips from the real battle: power gap, shots/possession, Power use, bench stars,
  Academy tickets, affordable Training); Comeback Roadmap after 3 final losses in a row. Rival strength never changes.
- REMATCH: one per fixture; squad, formation and Favorites can be changed before it; one final result per fixture.
- Trophy Cabinet (Division Champion, Direct Promotion, Promotion Survivor, Division 1 Championship, Champions League
  titles). First D1 title → Golden Champion Crest; Team Level 50 → Eternal Club Banner (the old Golden Crest
  conflict is resolved).
- Visual Club Evolution: 6 stages by highest Division unlock kit patterns (stripes, hoops, halves, sash, sleeves) and
  crest frames (silver, steel, gold, world, champion); the player's stands gain flags, banner, tifo, gold trim and
  stars (drawn once into the cached pitch). Cosmetic only.
- Progressive onboarding: short dismissible tips tied to real progress for the first 5 matches.

UI / PRESENTATION
- Mobile-first 5-tab navigation: Club (BATTLE, next rival, Training, identity, Trophy Cabinet, Club Journey) ·
  Squad (formations, active five, collection, Star Mastery, Academy) · Powers (24 cards, Favorites, mastery,
  unlock conditions) · League (table, fixtures, season milestones, Division Journey, Challenge Board + Glory Cup,
  history) · Rewards (Draft packs, Academy, earned-reward log).
- Battle HUD without command buttons: your loadout (Favorites ★ / Wilds ◆, lit while active), active star portraits
  (tap = Star Focus, ~5 s camera follow), one big Power notice at a time (rarity-styled; legendary flash), pause,
  1×/2×, smart-camera toggle, full screen. Smart camera capped at 1.22× (Action Focus on goals, epic/legendary
  Powers, miracle saves, rampages). Each Power has its own in-world visual and sound.
- Full-screen button kept in the top bar and on the battle screen.

SAVE
- Save schema v3 (same storage key, backup copy, validation, export/import). v1 and v2 careers migrate
  automatically with an explanatory notice: credits, fragments, XP/level, Division, fixtures, results, Training,
  stars (+OVR), packs, pity, challenges, endless history and claims are kept; the old real club keeps its name /
  colours as the custom profile (id renamed to PLAYER_CLUB in divisions, fixtures, results and claims) and becomes the
  Glory Cup guest; remaining contracts → Star XP (1 per 2, max 20); Contract Tickets → Academy Tickets.
- An unfinished Reborn or Mayhem battle resumes with its original rules and frozen teams (byte-identical replay).
